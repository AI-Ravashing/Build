How the tables connect

The spine runs left to right: a company at a show becomes a lead, the lead gets a brief, the brief becomes a project with an RV code, the project gets design versions and an estimate, and once won it drives purchasing and stock. Three links marked "by id" are kept as plain ids on purpose: Estimating, Purchasing and the Store write only their own tables and read the project by its id through the funnel.
