## At the Build area

1. **Job starts:** client PO and advance received, job number opened [B].
2. **Design released:** once quotation and design are final, Design releases the designs [Ali].
3. **PM collects** the technicals and BOQ [Ali].
4. **PM hands out technicals** to Carpentry, Electric, Paint and Graphics. Procurement also gets the BOQ [Ali].
5. **Procurement checks** the BOQ against stock, and Furniture's data for furniture, then orders what's missing. Item delays are rare [Ali].
6. **Carpentry and Graphics start together** [Ali]. Graphics also gets the brand logo, and makes a graphic mock-up and logo mock-up [Ali].
7. **Paint and Electric join** once some carpentry parts are ready: Paint applies primer, and Electric fits small electric items [Ali].
8. **Stand mock-up:** Carpentry completes it, with Electric and Paint done [Ali]. Water paint and spray paint are both done at the Build area [Ali].
9. **Logistics** loads everything once every department is ready, and takes it to the event [Ali]. It also moves people [A].

## Prateek's seam note, "The Seam Between Agency and Build" [PC2]

Four-page PDF dated 6 Nov 2026 (`NEED/Ravashing The Seam Between Agency and Build.pdf`). Prateek calls it not fully updated. Told, not confirmed by anyone in Build. Prateek also shared a combined final map (not yet seen by us).

- **Two halves, two systems, one seam.** Agency and Build keep their own maps. Almost everything crossing goes through the PM.
- **Four joints:** (1) Release to Production: the handover pack (locked design, approved technicals, print proofs, finish spec, build-affecting approvals, purchasing list), timestamped by the PM after the rep's go-ahead. (2) Coordination feed: venue slots, gate passes, approvals, from the PM down to Logistics. (3) Questions back: measurement doubts, delays, shortages, mock-up ready, via the PM, then the rep if the client is needed. (4) Procurement, on both sides, owns the stock list.
- **Rules:** the client talks only to the rep. Trades never call the designer; a doubt goes to the PM, who takes it to Design. Project Coordination reports to the PM, not to Logistics. Procurement owns the stock list; Furniture is part of its inventory. There is no per-stand BOQ: Procurement reads the 3D render. Every approval is the login that pressed it. When the maps disagree, the Agency map rules up to Release to Production and for the four joints; the Build map rules inside Build.
- **Mock-up and print proof are Agency work** under the Head of Design. The print itself is Build work.
- **The one missing loop:** site lessons back to Design through the PM does not exist today.
- **Workflow:** after release, Carpentry, Paint, Electric, Graphics print, then parts ready, warehouse assembly, pack and load list, logistics to venue, site build, handover, dismantle, returns to stock.

**Where it differs from what we had**
- It says Procurement is on both sides. Narendra says Prateek will take care of Procurement and Furniture as Agency. Open Questions 58.
- It says no per-stand BOQ. Ali said the PM sends the BOQ to Procurement. Open Questions 59.
- It says Coordinators report to the PM, who passes slots and passes to Logistics. We had assumed Coordinators talk to Logistics directly. Map changed: that flow now goes through the PM.
- It says "warehouse assembly". Narendra's word is Build area, not warehouse. Open Questions 61.

## Prateek's "Pain Points and Clashes, Agency vs Build" [PC3]

Six-page PDF, 6 Nov 2026 (`NEED/Ravashing Pain Points and Clashes, Agency vs Build.pdf`). Prateek laid our map (the earlier 24-flow version; its flow numbers are not today's) against his. It lists eight clashes and some pain points. Told by Prateek, not confirmed by anyone in Build. It states: "Ruling, agreed 6 Nov 2026: where the two maps disagree up to Release to Production or on the four joints, the Agency map rules. Inside the build, the Build map rules." Who agreed it is not stated.

**Clashes (Prateek's fix in brackets)**
- **C1 BOQ.** Our map: Design sends a BOQ, the PM passes it to Procurement (Ali, confirmed). Prateek: no per-stand BOQ exists; Purchasing reads the 3D render. (What Build calls "the BOQ" is probably the item and AV list inside the technicals; call it that.) A real conflict between two sources; settle by opening one real technicals pack together.
- **C2 Colour codes.** Our map: technicals carry colour codes (Ali). Prateek: colours are not on the drawings; Pantones are chased from the client mid-build. (Fix: a "Build Spec" in the DMS, one line per coloured element, required before release.) Paint and Spray Paint feel this.
- **C3 Client contact.** Our map: the client sends changes and sign-off straight to the PM, often in a WhatsApp group with Sales and the PM. Prateek: the client talks only to the rep; the rep logs a change order and the PM reads it, never receives it.
- **C4 Mock-ups.** Two things share one name. The design mock-up is Agency work (Head of Design, on the confirmed render). The print mock-up is the Branding trade checking the print proof fits the panels. Call them "design mock-up" and "print proof".
- **C5 Coordinators to Logistics.** Prateek: all coordination goes to the PM. Already redrawn on our map on 6 Nov.
- **C6 Furniture.** Prateek: a category inside Procurement's inventory, one stock list, one owner. Already moved to Agency on our map. Whether it stays a separate team is operations' call.
- **C7 Design doubts.** Both sides agree this is a leak. Prateek's fix: every doubt goes to the PM, who takes it to Design; the answer is written back on the Build Spec so Branding and Carpentry read the same change.
- **C8 After release.** Our map: the PM sends site changes to Accounts and lessons to Sales. Prateek: the Agency map ends at release and has no change order or final invoice step. His fix: a change-order record raised by the rep, priced by Estimation when drastic, summed into the final invoice.

**Pain points Prateek names:** the handover arrives in pieces (technicals by email, PO by email, dates and client changes by WhatsApp) and nothing says "released"; technicals reach trades as paper and a change after release does not reach the paper; the rep's go-ahead is a message, not a record; site status in WhatsApp and one Excel per PM; labour recorded almost nowhere; progress by phone; nobody sure who manages above the foremen; what Top Management looks at is unknown; lessons from site go to nobody.

**New facts and words:** Prateek's Agency side has a **DMS** (meaning unknown; a document system) and a **Build Spec** (planned, not confirmed as existing). Prateek rates our flows: 3 confirmed, 12 told, 9 guesses.

**His "confirm first" list, with today's flow names:** Coordinators to PM (who receives dates and passes, how); Coordinators to Logistics (decided on our map by his ruling, still unconfirmed by Logistics); PM to Furniture (who picks furniture, from which list); Procurement to PM (does the PM hear of a shortage before the trade?); Trades to Procurement (how often, who approves spend). Two sit-downs: open a real technicals pack and point at the BOQ; open the same pack and look for Pantones.

**Our caution:** Prateek's rulings describe how the Agency side *should* work. The people doing the work have said how it works *today*. The clashes C1 to C3 are not settled until a PM and a foreman look at a real pack. We keep the map as "today" and list the differences here.
