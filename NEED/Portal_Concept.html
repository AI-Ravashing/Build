Decisions for Prateek and Narendra

  1. Where does the latest design live? In Prateek's system or ours. We must keep one copy only; ours should link to his.
  2. Doubts: through the PM first, or to Design with the PM copied? The first may overload PMs on site.
  3. Foremen's progress: may foremen tap their own progress, instead of the PMs keeping it for them?
  4. Devices, languages, Wi-Fi: phones or a shared tablet? Which languages? Venue Wi-Fi may fail, so site use must work offline.
  5. Outsiders: may outside labour, AV and lighting companies, or hired trucks see a limited view?
  6. Alerts: WhatsApp, SMS or an app alert, and what the approved-tools list allows.

Where I would start

PMs, Carpentry and Branding, with one idea only: one current design, and who has seen it. Test it on two or three real stands. Habit is the main risk, not code.
