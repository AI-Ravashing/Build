# Where Build loses time, and how a portal could help

Written 6 Nov 2026, 16:58. **Ideas, not designs and not promises.** Research phase: nothing is built. Every finding is marked `told` (someone told Narendra), `Prateek` (his notes), `CONFIRMED` (someone doing the work said so) or `ASSUMED` (our guess from similar companies). Almost everything is `told` or `ASSUMED`, so each idea ends in a question for a real person. Agency work (Design, Sales, Procurement and the rest) is named where Build depends on it and set aside.

## 1. The six biggest findings

1. **Nobody holds one current picture of a stand.** A design change reached Carpentry but not the PM or Branding, and Branding kept the old design (told, Ali). Paper printouts make it worse: a change after release cannot reach the paper (Prateek).
2. **WhatsApp is doing the job of a system.** Status, site updates, dates, passes and client changes all live in chats (CONFIRMED, Ali). Chats cannot be searched by stand, have no "done" state and lose photos.
3. **The PMs are the relay for everything.** Four PMs plus Aman carry 10 or 20 stands each on a show day, and retype status to Sales and the top managers (told). Carpentry and Electric keep no data, so PMs keep it for them (told).
4. **The handover has no "released" moment.** Technicals by email, PO by email, dates by WhatsApp, go-ahead by message (Prateek). Build may also wait on technicals made after WON, and Pantones are chased from the client mid-build (Prateek).
5. **Nobody can see load or lateness early.** Aman cannot see all stands or each PM's load (ASSUMED, Aman not yet asked). A stand is known to be late only when someone shouts.
6. **Labour and equipment are invisible.** Own and outside labour is recorded almost nowhere. Lights, cables and AV reuse is untracked (ASSUMED).

## 2. How a portal would work, in plain English

**One file per stand.** Everything about a stand sits in one place: the current drawings, colours, graphics files, what each department must do, status, photos, doubts, changes and the load list. People open the stand, not a chat.

**Three ways in, by who you are.** The top managers get a board. Aman and the PMs get all stands and loads. A foreman or worker gets "My stands" with big buttons and almost no typing.

**WhatsApp stays, but as the doorbell, not the filing cabinet.** The portal sends a short alert (to WhatsApp if the approved-tools list allows it) with a link. Replies are done in the portal. This is how habits actually change: nobody has to stop using what they have; the important thing just moves.

**Statuses are taps, not sentences.** Ali's site steps (truck reached, offloaded, space identified, platform, electric, structure, putty, sanding, paint first coat, electric fitting, logo work) become a row of buttons. A tap gives the time, the person and the stand automatically.

**Photos belong to a step.** A photo taken at "mock-up done" or "packed" is attached to that stand and step, not sent to a chat.

**Every change is a record.** Whoever changes a design (Design, in their system) creates a change record that names what changed and who must see it. Each department must tap "Seen". Until a department taps, the PM sees them in red.

## 3. The hand-over from Agency to the PM and Build

The flow below follows Prateek's rules where he has stated them. Where I add something, it is marked.

1. **Design uploads** the technicals, colour spec (Pantone or "match to render"), print proofs and finish spec to the stand file. Design owns these; Build only reads them (see Decision A).
2. **Release gate.** The rep's go-ahead is a button press by the rep's login, not a message (Prateek). The PM checks a handover checklist: technicals, colours, print proofs, finish spec, approvals, purchasing list. Missing items are shown, not discovered later. The PM's tap stamps the release time.
3. **Each department sees only its part.** Carpentry sees structure drawings; Branding sees sizes and the zip's artwork; Water Paint and Spray Paint see colour per element; Electric sees plan and AV list. A "NEW" badge shows until they tap "Seen".
4. **A change after release** creates a change record (what, why, who asked). Everyone touching that stand gets an alert. The old drawing is marked "superseded". *Idea (mine):* every printed drawing carries a QR code; scanning it shows "current" or "replaced on 8 Nov", so paper can stay on the floor but cannot mislead.
5. **A doubt is a card.** The foreman taps "I have a doubt" on the drawing, adds a photo or marks the spot and a voice note. It goes to Design with the PM copied. Design's answer is written back on the drawing's page, so Carpentry, Branding and Paint read the same answer. If the answer changes the design, it becomes a change record (step 4) automatically. This is the missing "design doubt" path.
6. **Reverse flow to the PM.** The build schedule, site dates and client questions that Build must send back (Prateek) come from the stand's statuses and doubts, not from retyping.

## 4. Each department: what the head sees, what staff do, what is saved

*Team numbers are guesses or `told`. Devices (phone, tablet, shared screen) are unknown for every department: Decision D.*

### Project Management (four PMs plus Aman)
- **Head (Aman) sees:** all stands in a grid, with PMs as rows and weeks as columns. Each PM's load, stands that look late, doubts waiting, outside labour planned. A conflict flag when two stands need the same machine or people in the same week.
- **PM sees:** "My stands", one card each, with a status colour, next step, open doubts, unseen changes and the last photo. On site, a walk checklist per stand in step order.
- **What they stop doing:** typing status into a personal Excel and then into WhatsApp for Sales and the boss; chasing "did you get the new drawing?"; asking Logistics about trucks. The system sends Sales and the top managers a status page instead.
- **Saved:** hours of retyping each week (honest guess: 30 to 60 minutes per PM per day; needs a real count). A common stand list for all PMs (today there is no standard).
- **Ask to confirm:** how many stands per PM, what Aman needs on one screen, who "the boss" is.

### Carpentry (foreman, head carpenter, helpers)
- **Head sees:** stands due, the current drawings, changes not yet seen, the doubts they raised and their answers.
- **Staff see:** a large card per stand: "what to build now", drawing, a "done" button per part, a photo button.
- **They stop:** asking the PM or Design by phone which drawing is right; the PM writing their progress for them.
- **Saved:** the old-design mistake, which is the best-evidenced problem we have.
- **Ask:** whether the foreman could keep progress himself (today the PMs do; Narendra's idea) or whether that is a burden. Also which machine the head carpenter runs and where the CNC sits.

### Water Paint and Spray Paint
- **Head sees:** which parts are ready for them, colour per element, which stands need spraying versus water paint, booth schedule (spray only).
- **Staff see:** part, colour code and finish, a "done" tap, a photo of the finish.
- **They stop:** asking for colours; hearing late that a colour changed. **Colour is the main prize:** a colour spec per element removes the Pantone chasing (Prateek), and a colour change reaches Paint as an alert.
- **Saved:** repainting and mid-build calls to the client.
- **Ask:** how paint is used and recorded; what limits the spray booth; who works on site; how Paint and Spray share a stand.

### Electric (foreman, 7 electricians, outsourced AV and lighting)
- **Head sees:** plan, list, equipment per stand, who is outside booked, what is not yet powered on site.
- **Staff see:** per stand a checklist: fit, test, power on, with a photo of the test.
- **They stop:** the PM tracking them by walking. Outsourced AV and lighting companies could get a limited view of only their stands (Decision E).
- **Saved:** the PM walking stand to stand just to learn Electric's status; and a record of what equipment went where, which makes reuse possible (ASSUMED reuse is worth tracking).
- **Ask:** who books outside AV and lighting; whether they would use a link.

### Branding (Graphics, CNC, Logo)
- **Head sees:** the Design zip file in one place, print queue, sizes, mock-up approvals, which files are final.
- **Staff see:** per panel: size, file, "printed", "packed", "fitted" with a photo.
- **They stop:** getting a zip by chat; asking Design about measurements by phone; printing twice because a size changed. A size or file change shows as a change record.
- **Saved:** reprints. *Idea (mine):* a size check when a graphic is marked printed ("matches the current drawing? yes or no"), so a panel printed to an old size is caught before packing.
- **Ask:** how the zip arrives, who approves the mock-up, and whether the design mock-up and the print proof are separate steps.

### Logistics (not yet reached; everything here is ASSUMED)
- **Head sees:** stands ready to load, truck bookings, venue slots and passes (arriving via the PM), furniture and stock to load (the PM arranges, even before site), load lists.
- **Staff see:** a load list per truck on the phone, with ticks as items go on.
- **They stop:** paper load lists; "ready?" calls; the PM asking whether the truck has left. *Idea (mine):* QR labels on crates; scanning at loading, at the site and at offload gives "truck left, reached, offloaded" with no one typing it. It also shows what is missing at site before the PM finds out.
- **Saved:** the "where is it?" calls, which are a large share of PM interruptions on show days (ASSUMED).
- **Ask:** anything. Logistics is the least known department.

### Top managers (three people)
- **See:** a board of stands by colour, which shows are coming, and which stands are risky (late, many changes, many doubts). Nothing to type. They stop receiving updates by message.
- **Ask:** what each of them looks at (not known).

## 6. What I would do first (Phase 5 candidate, not approved)

Per the approved plan, Build must run on manual job intake and not wait for Stand Studio. A small start follows from the evidence:

- **Slice 1: "One current design, and who has seen it."** Stand file, latest drawings (a link to wherever Design keeps them), change records, "Seen" taps, doubt cards. Pilot with PMs, Carpentry and Branding only. It answers the one mistake we are sure of.
- **Slice 2: status taps and photos** for the same departments, plus a board for Aman and the top managers.
- **Slice 3: Logistics** load lists and QR scans.
- **Slice 4: labour counts and equipment**, only if the earlier slices stick.

Honest estimate: I cannot give build time yet, because we do not know devices, languages, Wi-Fi at venues, or where Design keeps its files. The bigger risk is habit, not code: four PMs and a handful of foremen must prefer a tap to a message. A pilot on two or three real stands is the real test. Ali's team is small enough to try.

## 7. Decisions that belong to people

- **A. Where does the "latest design" live?** Prateek's side (his DMS, Stand Studio) or ours? We must not keep two copies. Ours should link to his. Ask Prateek.
- **B. Doubts: through the PM first, or to Design with the PM copied?** Prateek's rule says through the PM. That may overload PMs on site (Open Question 14). My preference: Design with the PM copied and visible, but it is his rule.
- **C. May foremen keep their own progress?** Narendra's idea; unconfirmed.
- **D. Devices, language, Wi-Fi.** Who has a phone? Is a shared tablet at the Build area better? Which languages must the screens support? Venue Wi-Fi may fail, so site use needs to work offline.
- **E. May outsiders (outside labour, AV and lighting, hired trucks) see a limited view?** (Prateek's list item.)
- **F. Alerts.** WhatsApp, SMS or a phone notification, and what the approved-tools list allows.
- **G. Photos and voice notes.** Who may see them; rules for client and venue images.

## 8. What would make these ideas wrong

- The old-design mistake may be rare. Count how often it happens.
- PMs may not retype as much as I think. Watch one PM for a day.
- A foreman may not want a phone app at all. Ask Jason and Happy.
- Prateek's system may already cover some of this. Ask him what Stand Studio will do.
- A tap-based status may be gamed (people tap "done" early). The answer is photos, not more rules.
