# Questions: Electric

Round 1 only. Star = ask first. Ask about the last real stand. Short talk, not a form. Questions already answered by what we were told are left out (see `WORKING_UNDERSTANDING.md`). Generated from `Build_Map.html`; edit it there.

1. ★ [People] Who gives you your daily work: the PM or Production Coordination? Who are the outside AV and lighting people, who books them, and how many do we use at the busiest time? When the six electricians are all busy, who does the extra work?
2. [Work] What do you fit at the Build area, and what is left for site? Who makes the plan for sockets and lights, and how do you test your work?
3. ★ [Flows] How do you know a stand is ready for you? If the electrical plan changes, how do you hear? Which departments do you talk to, and how?
4. ★ [Records] On site, does anyone besides the PM note your progress? Which lights, cables and AV items do we own, reuse or hire, and who tracks where they are? How do you ask Procurement for material?
5. [Capacity] How many stands at once? Who decides which goes first, and when do you use outside electricians? Who connects to the venue power and tests before opening?
6. ★ [Problem] What went wrong most recently, and who noticed first? What one thing do you wish you could see quickly?
