# Questions: Water Paint

Round 1 only. Star = ask first. Ask about the last real stand. Short talk, not a form. Questions already answered by what we were told are left out (see `WORKING_UNDERSTANDING.md`). Generated from `Build_Map.html`; edit it there.

1. ★ [People] Who heads Water Paint, and is there a manager above him? How many people are in the team (ours and outside, normally and at the busiest)? Who keeps the labour information?
2. [Work] Take your last stand, from the first parts arriving to finished. What do you paint (water paint and primer), and how is it different from spray paint? What is done here, and what is left for site?
3. ★ [Flows] How do you know a part is ready for you, and where do the colours come from? If the colour changes, how do you hear? Which departments do you talk to, and how?
4. ★ [Records] What do you write down: paint used, colours, who worked, hours? Who keeps these records, and where? Can you show me?
5. [Capacity] How many stands at once? Who decides which goes first, and when do you use outside painters? Who paints on site, and who briefs them on colours? How do you work with Spray Paint?
6. ★ [Problem] What went wrong most recently, and who noticed first? What one thing do you wish you could see quickly?
