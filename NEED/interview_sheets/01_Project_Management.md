# Questions: Project Management (PMs) and Aman, head of PMs

Round 1 only. Star = ask first. Ask about the last real stand. Short talk, not a form. Questions already answered by what we were told are left out (see `WORKING_UNDERSTANDING.md`). Generated from `Build_Map.html`; edit it there.

1. ★ [People] How many stands does one PM handle at a time, normally and at the most? Who looks after your stands when you are at another show? How many outside PMs do we hire at the busiest time, and who arranges them?
2. ★ [Flows] What do you receive from Design, Sales and the Coordinators (files, dates, passes)? How does each one reach you? Open one real technicals pack with me: what do you call the BOQ in it, and are colour codes (Pantones) on it? Do you check a handover pack before you release to Production: what is in it, who is the rep who gives the go-ahead, and what happens if something is missing?
3. [Flows] What exactly do you hand to each trade and to Logistics? How do you arrange furniture and stock with Logistics (before the site too), and how do you pass on the venue slots and passes from the Coordinators? When something changes, how do you tell them? What do you send back to the rep and the client: build schedule, site dates, questions?
4. ★ [Records] When you update Sales and the boss, who is the boss here, how do you send it (WhatsApp?) and who writes it down? Can you show me your Excel and one WhatsApp group for a stand? What is in each column, who else can see them, and where do the photos go?
5. [Work] At the Build area, how do you know how far each stand has got? How early can you tell it will be late?
6. [Site] On site with 10 or 20 stands: how many PMs are there, how do you note each step, and who guides the workers at a stand when you are at another one? How do you brief outside labour or outside PMs?
7. ★ [Problem] Is it normal for a foreman to talk to Design directly? What would stop the old-design mistake from happening again, without everything having to go through you?
8. [Close] How does a stand end: handover sheet, sign-off, photos, and what you tell Accounts about site changes? And what one thing do you wish you could see on your phone each morning?
