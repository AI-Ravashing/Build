# Questions: Logistics (Transport)

Round 1 only. Star = ask first. Ask about the last real stand. Short talk, not a form. Questions already answered by what we were told are left out (see `WORKING_UNDERSTANDING.md`). Generated from `Build_Map.html`; edit it there.

1. ★ [People] The chart shows Transport as 7 people. Does Transport do only trucks and loading, or also site work? Who plans the trucks and keeps the bookings? Are the vehicles ours or hired, and who do you take work from: the PM or Production Coordination?
2. [Work] Take the last stand you sent. How did you know it was ready, who made the load list, and who checked it at the site?
3. ★ [Flows] Do you get venue slots and passes from the PM or from the Coordinators? How do the PM and you arrange furniture and stock to load? How does the PM learn the truck has left, reached and offloaded? Which other departments do you talk to?
4. ★ [Records] What do you write down: bookings, load lists, costs for each stand? Who writes it, and where? Is there one place where all coming site dates can be seen?
5. [After] Does your work stop at delivery, or do you help build? Who is in charge at site? After the show, what comes back, who checks it, and who removes the scrap and at what cost?
6. [Overseas] For jobs outside the UAE, how does the stand get there? Who handles shipping and customs, and who travels?
7. ★ [Problem] How many stands do you move in the same week, and who decides which goes first? What went wrong most recently?
