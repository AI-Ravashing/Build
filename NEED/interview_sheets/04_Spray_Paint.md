# Questions: Spray Paint

Round 1 only. Star = ask first. Ask about the last real stand. Short talk, not a form. Questions already answered by what we were told are left out (see `WORKING_UNDERSTANDING.md`). Generated from `Build_Map.html`; edit it there.

1. ★ [People] Who is the foreman, and is there a manager above him? How many people are in the team (ours and outside, normally and at the busiest)? Why is spray paint a separate department from Paint?
2. [Work] Take your last stand: when do parts reach you, where do you spray, and how long do they dry? Is the spray area or drying time ever a limit? What makes you spray on site in the rare cases, and who decides?
3. ★ [Flows] How do you know a part is ready for you, and who gives you the colours? If a colour changes, how do you hear? How do you work with Paint and Carpentry, and who do you tell when you finish?
4. ★ [Records] What do you write down: paint used, colours, who worked, hours? Who keeps these records, and where? Can you show me?
5. [Capacity] How many stands at once? Who decides which goes first, and when do you use outside spray painters?
6. ★ [Problem] What went wrong most recently, and who noticed first? What one thing do you wish you could see quickly?
