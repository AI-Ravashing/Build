How it stays light for people

Nine rules, and a time budget for each role. The budget is a target to test in a pilot, not a promise.

Nine rules

  1. Enter once, at the source: forms start pre-filled from Stand Studio.
  2. Arrival is automatic: a recorded delivery tells the trade its material is here.
  3. Relevance, not broadcast: a change goes only to departments whose elements changed.
  4. Quiet by default: routine news in one daily digest; instant only if it changes someone's current work.
  5. One nudge, then escalate to the PM's board. No repeats.
  6. Crews need no logins: foremen tap for them.
  7. Works on site: taps and photos queue and send when there is a signal.
  8. The PM is the screen, not the relay.
  9. Link to Design's files; never copy them.
