Site steps a PM tracks, walking from stand to stand

Handoff, from Prateek's chart: after WON four tracks run before release. Technicals (Design: 2D drawings, finishes). Coordination (organisers, venues, approvals, passes; waits for the technicals). Graphics (sizes via the rep, mock-up, print proof). Purchasing (from the 3D render: stock or new to buy). The PM checks the handover pack, the rep gives the go-ahead, and the PM releases to Production.
